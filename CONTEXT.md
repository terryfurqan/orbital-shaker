# DIY Orbital Shaker

Glossary and canonical domain concepts for the DIY Digital Orbital Shaker (25x30 cm table, 1.5 kg capacity, up to 300 RPM).

## Mechanical & Kinematics

**Orbital Motion**:
A pure circular translation of the platform where every point on the surface traces a circle of identical diameter without self-rotation.
_Avoid_: Rotary motion, spinning, tumbling

**Orbit Radius (Eccentric Offset)**:
The distance from the center of rotation to the eccentric pin axis (commonly $r = 10\text{ mm}$, yielding a 20 mm orbit stroke diameter).
_Avoid_: Stroke length, throw distance

**Satellite Idler**:
A passive eccentric bearing unit mounted at the perimeter to maintain parallel orientation of the platform during orbital motion.
_Avoid_: Corner roller, slave pulley

**Central Drive Hub**:
The primary driven eccentric cam connected directly or via transmission to the motor shaft.
_Avoid_: Rotor hub, center crank

**Counterweight**:
A balancing mass placed 180 degrees opposite the eccentric offset to neutralize centrifugal dynamic forces generated by the rotating platform and sample liquid.
_Avoid_: Ballast, deadweight, stabilizer

**Top Plate**:
The moving top surface fixture carrying the vessels/bottles and clamping mechanisms.
_Avoid_: Bed, tray, shaker table

## Electrical & Motion Control

**Silent Stepper Driver**:
A motor driver utilizing ultra-quiet sinusoidal current chopper algorithms (such as TMC2209 StealthChop2) to eliminate acoustic motor resonance.
_Avoid_: Standard driver, A4988 board

**Step Generation Rate**:
The frequency of pulse interrupts generated by the microcontroller to maintain target RPM under microstepping.
_Avoid_: Clock tick, pulse speed

## Hardware Decision & Architecture

- **Motor**: NEMA 17 (40 mm body length, 17HS4401, Holding Torque ~40-42 N·cm, 1.5A).
- **Driver**: MKS TMC2209 V2.0 / V1.3 (StealthChop2, disetel Vref ~0.9V - 1.1V).
- **Power Supply**: PSU Switching Jaring 24V 5A (120W).
- **Power Distribution**:
  - Jalur 24V: Menyuplai pin VMOT pada TMC2209 (untuk tenaga putaran stepper 300 RPM).
  - Jalur Step-down LM2596 (Output disetel 12V): Menyuplai Kipas Brushless 12V 5010 dan pin VIN Arduino Uno (internal regulator Arduino menghasilkan 5V untuk modul sensor/display).
- **Cooling**: Kipas DC 12V 5010 diarahkan langsung meniup heatsink TMC2209.
- **Controller & I/O**:
  - MCU: Arduino Uno R3.
  - Input: Rotary Encoder KY-040 (fine tuning RPM/Menu) + Keypad Matriks 4x4 (input angka cepat).
  - Output: LCD 1602 (wajib modul I2C PCF8574 agar hemat pin).
- **Motion Ramp**: Firmware wajib menerapkan profil akselerasi bertahap (ramp-up 1-2 detik) agar motor tidak stall akibat inersia cairan 1.5 kg.
